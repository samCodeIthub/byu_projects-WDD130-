Our Trips

Half Day Adventure

Perfect for beginners and families. This 4-hour trip takes you through gentle Class II rapids with stunning canyon views. All gear and a certified guide are included.

Duration: 4 hours | Level: Beginner | Price: $75/person

Full Day Adventure

A full day on the river tackling Class III and IV rapids. Includes lunch on the riverbank, all safety equipment, and an experienced guide. Great for thrill seekers.

Duration: 8 hours | Level: Intermediate | Price: $130/person

Weekend Adventure

Two days of rafting combined with riverside camping under the stars. Tackle Class IV and V rapids and enjoy guided nature walks. A true wilderness experience.

Duration: 2 days | Level: Advanced | Price: $280/person
